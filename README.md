# 🎁 Photo Gifts Quotation & Inventory Analysis | Excel

An Excel-based **customer quotation and inventory analysis tool** designed for a photo gifts business.

The project combines **product lookup, customer discounts, multi-currency pricing, quotation automation, and inventory monitoring** into one practical Excel workflow.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The workbook helps a business create customer quotations while automatically retrieving product information, applying relevant discounts, converting prices into different currencies, and identifying products that may require replenishment.

### Key Business Questions

* What product has the customer selected?
* What is the current product price?
* Which discount should the customer receive?
* What is the quotation value in the selected currency?
* Which products are running low on stock?
* How much stock should be reordered?
